Surface-guided Respiratory Tracking

Within a large national radiotherapy research program, I own the workstream that tracks patient breathing with 3D surface imaging and registers it to 4DCT so treatment guidance can respect respiratory motion.

Radiotherapy targets move with breathing. The program explores surface-guided tracking so the delivery system can follow or gate on respiratory motion; my assignment connects the optical surface signal to the 4DCT respiratory phases.

Decision Timeline

Uncertainty

Can an optical surface signal stand in for internal respiratory phase?

Surface motion is observable without dose, but the mapping from external surface to internal 4DCT phase must be established and validated before any guidance claim.

Probe

I am building the tracking and registration pipeline as a testable path — surface acquisition, respiratory signal extraction, and phase alignment against 4DCT — before integrating with the wider delivery system.

Evidence

Phase agreement between the surface signal and 4DCT will be measured on recorded datasets first; no accuracy figure is reported until that measurement exists.
