Decision Timeline

Uncertainty

Which inspection decisions could be moved into simulation?

A detailed 3D scene would be useless unless it reduced uncertainty about routes, sensors, and operator actions.

Probe

Model one inspection scenario first

I connected a representative robot, sensors, and constrained route rather than attempting full facility coverage.

Evidence

Coverage and route conflicts became visible

The simulated viewpoint showed where sensing assumptions and navigation clearances needed revision.

Decision

Use the twin as a rehearsal surface

I centered the prototype on decisions an operator or developer could inspect, not on photorealism.

Integration

Connect robot state and visualization

The environment, robot interface, and sensor feedback were combined into a coherent remote-inspection demonstrator.

Verified Outcome

Representative inspection flow demonstrated

The prototype supported controlled rehearsal of a remote-inspection scenario and made route and sensing assumptions reviewable.

My Decisions

  • Selected the representative scenario.
  • Prioritized route and sensing decisions over visual fidelity.
  • Integrated robot and sensor feedback into the twin.

Team Result

  • The research team gained a shared inspection demonstrator.
  • Scenario assumptions could be reviewed before physical access.

Current Status

Completed research demonstrator. The evidence supports scenario rehearsal in simulation, not production operation or safety certification in a live hazardous facility.
