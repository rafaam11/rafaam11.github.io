Decision Timeline

Uncertainty

Would two taps create a usable measurement?

Tracking drift and ambiguous surface selection could make a precise-looking number misleading.

Probe

Add visible anchors and a connecting line

I tested direct point placement with persistent visual feedback rather than showing only the numeric result.

Evidence

Users could inspect point selection

Anchors and the line made accidental surface choices visible and allowed a measurement to be reset.

Decision

Show the measurement construction

I kept point markers and the segment visible so the result retained its spatial context.

Integration

Package the flow on-device

Plane tracking, hit testing, anchors, distance calculation, units, and reset behavior were combined in one mobile interaction.

Verified Outcome

Two-point measurement demonstrated

The prototype completed the intended measure-and-reset flow on a supported mobile AR device.
