At a glance

The prototype asked how planned jaw movement and clinically relevant references could be understood spatially instead of as disconnected measurements.

My responsibility

Evidence

State

Decision Timeline

Uncertainty

Which planning references matter in 3D?

A generic anatomical viewer would not communicate the occlusal and movement decisions used in orthognathic planning.

Probe

Visualize one planning transition

I built a small AR scene showing before/after jaw relationships and selected clinical references.

Evidence

The movement narrative was easier to inspect

Reviewers could point to placement and sequence issues directly in the 3D view.

Decision

Center interaction on planning intent

I reduced decorative interaction and emphasized jaw movement, midline, and bite relationship.

Integration

Package the sequence as a reviewable prototype

The clinical references, 3D anatomy, and interaction flow were combined into one demonstration.

Verified Outcome

Planning concept demonstrated

The prototype provided a concrete basis for expert discussion of the AR planning workflow.

My Decisions

Team Result

  • Clinical collaborators could review the concept in 3D.
  • The team gained clearer requirements for subsequent XR work.

Current Status

Completed research prototype. It supports workflow discussion, not surgical accuracy, treatment outcome, or regulatory claims.
