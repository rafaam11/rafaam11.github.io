Oral & Facial Anatomy AR

A compact AR prototype for placing clinically meaningful oral and facial anatomy into an explorable learning sequence.

Research 2023.08 – 2023.12 · Educational XR

UnityMobile AR3D Anatomy

At a glance

The design problem was deciding what anatomical relationships should be revealed together and what interaction would help inspection without overwhelming the learner.

My responsibility
Content structure, 3D interaction, AR implementation
Evidence
Working educational prototype
State
Research demonstrator

Decision Timeline

Uncertainty

How much anatomy belongs in one AR view?

Showing every structure at once reduced the ability to understand relationships.

Probe

Test layered reveal and direct manipulation

I prototyped a small set of anatomical layers with rotate, scale, isolate, and label interactions.

Evidence

Layering clarified spatial relationships

The simplified sequence made it easier to compare neighboring structures than a single dense model.

Decision

Organize by learning relationship

I grouped content around what should be understood together, rather than model-file hierarchy.

Integration

Build the sequence into mobile AR

Models, labels, layer controls, and spatial manipulation were combined into a portable demonstrator.

Verified Outcome

Educational interaction demonstrated

The finished prototype supported an explorable oral and facial anatomy sequence on a mobile AR device.

My Decisions
