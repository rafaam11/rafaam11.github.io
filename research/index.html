Transferable Capabilities

How I reduce 3D system uncertainty

My work moves across medical XR, robotics, and industrial autonomy, but the underlying job stays consistent: define the coordinate problem, make uncertainty visible, gather evidence, and integrate only what survives validation.

01

Geometry foundation

3D Registration & Navigation

What I solve

Transform camera, medical-image, tracker, and world coordinates into a traceable spatial chain that supports navigation rather than a convincing-looking overlay.

How I validate

I derive the transforms, isolate each SDK or tracker dependency in a micro-PoC, visualize residual errors, and verify the full chain against domain-specific scenarios.

Evidence: Surgical Twin · rTMS Navigation · Mandibular Fracture Navigation · C-arm Navigation

02

Sensing under constraints

Sensor Perception & Safety

What I solve

Determine where depth, vision, and obstacle sensors are trustworthy—and how their uncertainty should change system behavior.

How I validate

I expose regions of interest, noise waveforms, blind spots, coordinate frames, and safety-gate decisions through visual overlays before field deployment.

Evidence: Unmanned Forklift · Quadruped Robot

03

Evidence before field cost

Digital Twin & Validation

What I solve

Move expensive, slow, or hazardous verification into a repeatable environment without pretending simulation replaces reality.

How I validate

I encode the decisions that matter—sensor coverage, safety gates, coordinate behavior, or route constraints—then use field tests to close the remaining gap.

Evidence: Radioactive Facility Digital Twin · LiteSim for Unmanned Forklift

04

Domain translation

Clinical-to-XR Translation

What I solve

Convert clinical language, instrument workflows, and anatomical decision points into interaction flows and spatial software requirements.

How I validate

I show working 3D prototypes early, confirm the scenario with domain experts, and progress through approval, adoption, or research validation as appropriate.

Evidence: Life Careverse · Orthognathic AR · Oral & Facial AR · AR Distance Meter

05

Engineering leverage

AI-Enabled Tool Building

What I solve

Remove the UI, binding, data-structure, and repetitive implementation bottlenecks that delay an engineering decision.

How I validate

I retain human ownership of context, requirements, architecture, acceptance criteria, and PR review while agents accelerate implementation and documentation.

Evidence: LLM Wiki · sensor-validation tools · PR and planning governance used by the development team
