Robotics & Computer Vision R&D Engineer

Surgical Navigation · 3D Registration · Optical Tracking · Computer Vision · XR · Robotics Integration

I connect anatomy, surgical instruments, sensors, cameras, XR devices, and robots in one 3D coordinate space. Through more than three years of collaborative R&D with the oral and maxillofacial surgery team at Samsung Medical Center, I develop image-to-patient registration, surgical instrument tracking, and digital occlusion workflows.

My work grew from optical tracking and coordinate registration into surgical navigation and XR, and from there into robot and vision system integration.
