Marker-Based AR Distance Meter

A personal Android prototype that calculates the three-dimensional distance between two Vuforia-tracked marker positions.

Completed 2021.03 – 2021.09 · Geometry experiment

UnityVuforiaAndroidC#Linear Algebra

At a glance

The core task was to express two tracked marker centers in one Unity world frame and make their Euclidean distance visible on an Android device.

Evidence

Decision Timeline

Uncertainty

Could two marker poses produce one meaningful 3D distance?

Each tracked object had its own transform, so both positions needed to be compared in a shared frame.

Probe

Visualize both marker centers and their vector

I first displayed the tracked positions and connecting displacement before presenting a scalar distance.

Evidence

Marker movement changed the shared-frame displacement

Device tests showed the two tracked transforms updating in the Unity world coordinate system.

Decision

Use world-space Euclidean distance

I calculated the norm of the difference between marker positions and kept the marker relationship visible.

Integration

Package tracking, geometry, and display on Android

Vuforia tracking, Unity transforms, distance calculation, and the result UI were combined into one application.

Verified Outcome

Marker-to-marker measurement demonstrated

The completed prototype displayed the three-dimensional distance between two tracked markers on Android.
