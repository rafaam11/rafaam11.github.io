Multi-sensor Registration for an Autonomous Forklift

Connected Jetson-based ToF–RGB vision (two-stage calibration, SAM3 segmentation, quality gates, continuous truck-load tracking), vision-servoing actions, a LiDAR sensor-integration service, and the initial safety-manager design into one integration flow on Zenoh-based in-house middleware.

Vehicle-control, localisation, mission, safety, and field-validation owners integrate the system jointly. The middleware is an in-house Zenoh-based implementation that follows ROS 2-style patterns.

The public clip and photos are test-site runs. The measurements are internal development measurements; no production or customer outcomes are claimed.
