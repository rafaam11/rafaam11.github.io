    • Delivered several development contracts for research institutes including KERI and KIMM.

    Robotics & Vision — Logistics Robots and Autonomous Forklift (2024.12 - )

    • Extending optical tracking and coordinate registration experience to robot–camera coordinate integration, robot vision and sensor integration.
    • Since 2025.11, designed and initially implemented the safety manager of the in-house autonomous-forklift solution (DOTORI) — three-zone virtual bumper and E-STOP latching policy — and integrated LiDAR and ToF sensors into the Zenoh-based in-house middleware with a sensor-integration service.
    • DOTORI vision R&D — production vision on Jetson (two-stage ToF↔RGB calibration, SAM3 segmentation, quality gates, continuous truck-load tracking) and the earlier Windows prototype; measured ToF↔RGB synchronisation at p95 16.6 ms.
    • Integrated vision servoing (wheel-pair detection → fork alignment, approach and insertion) and loading/unloading actions into the team's behaviour trees; contributed to the web-based operator and monitoring app UI and to Isaac Sim simulation work.
    • Before DOTORI — built the map editor of a Unity-based fleet management system for logistics robots (2024.12–2025.01) and a drone marker-tracking prototype linking a mobile drone-SDK app with PC-side marker tracking (2025.08–09).
